Mallard Protocol

A short audiovisual trip with a duck in it. Put headphones on or turn the phone sideways: the sound moves left to right.

  • Loud bass and sudden squeaks.
  • Moving patterns and colour changes. It never strobes faster than twice a second, and every mode stays under the three-flashes-per-second limit.
  • Some people feel dizzy or sick from moving patterns. Stop if you do.

If you have photosensitive epilepsy or are prone to migraines, choose Calm.
