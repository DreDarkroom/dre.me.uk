Why hands, not dice?

Nothing a human does is truly random. People asked to make up a random sequence avoid repeating themselves (Wagenaar, 1972, Psychological Bulletin 77, 65–72), and the tiny timing wobbles in real playing are not white noise either: in Hennig et al. (2011, PLoS ONE) they had long-range correlations, and listeners preferred that to plain random jitter.

So this game avoids plain dice. The seed comes from your scribble. "Hand dice" avoid repeats the way people do. "Human feel" drifts the timing in a correlated way, then you can hear the difference against white-noise jitter.
