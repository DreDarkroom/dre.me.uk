Listen to the target beat, then switch on the same steps.

How to play

  1. Press Play target and listen. It loops until you stop it.
  2. Tap squares to switch drum hits on. Each row is a drum, and each square is a beat in the bar from left to right.
  3. Press Check. Green squares are right. A red dashed × is a hit that shouldn't be there.
  4. Stuck? Press Hint. Each press gives a bigger clue.
